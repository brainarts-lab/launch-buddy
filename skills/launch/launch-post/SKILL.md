---
name: launch-post
description: Write a launch post for X from a README or notes, at most 280 characters, ready to paste.
---

1. First line: what people can do now. No "Excited to announce", no emoji opener.
2. Second part: the one detail that makes it believable, taken from the material
   (how it works, what it leaves out, what it costs).
3. Last line: how to try it, followed by the placeholder [link].
4. At most 280 characters including the placeholder. Count before answering and state the count.
5. At most one hashtag, and only if the material names a community or event.
6. Never invent numbers, testimonials or availability.

On request, expand into a thread of up to five posts: problem, what it does, how it
works, what it does not do, how to try it.
