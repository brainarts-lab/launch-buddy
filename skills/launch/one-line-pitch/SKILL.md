---
name: one-line-pitch
description: Boil a project down to one line of at most twelve words that says who it is for and what they get.
---

1. Find the one thing a person can do with the project that they could not do as easily before.
2. Name who it is for in plain words, or leave the audience out when it is obvious.
3. Write one line of at most twelve words. Start with a verb or with the result.
4. No product category words ("platform", "solution", "framework") unless the material uses them as the name.
5. No claim the material does not support.

Check: could a stranger repeat the line to a friend after reading it once? If not, shorten it.
